Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Applies to the CareOpsDesk Publisher app and the CareOpsDesk social media accounts

CareOpsDesk is a small online shop on Etsy that sells editable business document templates. The CareOpsDesk Publisher app is used only by CareOpsDesk itself, to publish its own videos and posts to its own Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel. It is not offered to other businesses or the public.

What the app accesses

  • The CareOpsDesk Facebook Page and its linked Instagram professional account (their IDs, names and follower counts).
  • The CareOpsDesk YouTube channel (its ID, name and the videos the app uploads), through the YouTube API Services.
  • The posts the app publishes (post IDs, links and basic performance figures such as views and likes).
  • Comments the app itself writes under those posts (for example, a link to the product on Etsy).

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data of people who view, like or comment on our posts.
  • It does not log in other users, read private messages, or access anyone else's accounts.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data, and it does not use data for advertising or profiling.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services to upload CareOpsDesk's own videos to its own channel and to read their view counts. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in a private repository and on the owner's computer, and are never published. Publishing records (post IDs and links) are kept so the same video is never posted twice.

Purchases on Etsy

Orders, payments and buyer information are handled by Etsy under Etsy's Privacy Policy. CareOpsDesk only sees what Etsy shares with sellers to deliver an order.

Your choices and data deletion

If you believe the app holds information about you and you want it removed, follow the steps on our data deletion page. We respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
