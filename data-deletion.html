Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The CareOpsDesk Publisher app does not log in users and does not store personal data of people who view or comment on our posts. If you still want us to check for and delete any information related to you:

  1. Send us a message through our Etsy shop: etsy.com/shop/CareOpsDesk (use "Message seller"), with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Tell us the Facebook or Instagram name the request is about.
  3. We will delete any related information we hold and confirm within 30 days.

You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Facebook settings: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
